## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-precision, tactical diagnostic environment tailored for advanced cognitive scaffolding and adaptive learning. Moving far beyond generic commercial software patterns, it draws direct inspiration from aerospace head-up displays (HUD), Rainmeter desktop instrumentation, and military telemetry consoles. The interface channels the persona of an omnipresent, hyper-intelligent computational tutor: quiet, surgical, unyielding, and acutely focused on raw signal clarity.

The visual style fuses technical brutalism with tactical glassmorphism and instrument-grade telemetry. Surfaces do not rely on standard rounded cards or blurred chromatic gradients. Instead, structural density is driven by hairline reticles, orbital radial arcs, segmented angular brackets, tick-mark scales, and strict orthographic layouts. The overall emotional response should evoke focused calm, intellectual supremacy, and total operational control—transforming learning mastery into an analytical mission.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around pure visual contrast against deep cosmic absorption, avoiding soft pastel tones and generic purple gradients entirely:

- **Deep Field Foundations:** Canvas grounding is built on absolute dark slate (`#090B0D`), tiered upward into matte technical obsidian (`#111519`) and pressurized graphite (`#171D22`).
- **Primary Energy:** Electric cyan (`#00D9FF`) serves as the active signal emitter, supported by illumination tiers (`#79E8FF` for hot glow nodes, `#B7F4FF` for pinpoint vector reticles, and `#005366` for dormant background loops).
- **Secondary Telemetry:** Muted amber (`#D7A65A`) functions as the auxiliary telemetry channel, dedicated to alerts, system friction, warnings, and divergent learning paths.
- **Structural Framing:** Crisp hairlines operate at precise micro-opacities via technical slate (`#1E2830`) and energized boundary steel (`#263844`).
- **Readout Spectrum:** Text values strictly prioritize legibility under dark adaptation, utilizing crisp phosphor white (`#E6F7FF`), muted cyan-gray (`#8EA7B5`), and subdued chassis carbon (`#4D6270`).

## Typography

Typography operates as dual-register instrumentation:

1. **System Telemetry & Metadata (`JetBrains Mono`):** All labels, readouts, coordinates, dynamic statuses, and metric counters are rendered in monospaced uppercase. Wide tracking (`0.08em` to `0.14em`) preserves legibility in micro-scales across low-luminosity states.
2. **Cognitive Synthesis & Academic Prose (`Manrope`):** Narrative problem prompts, theoretical breakdowns, and instructional dialogue use clean, geometric sans-serif styling with optimized line heights to guarantee effortless readability during prolonged study cycles.

Headings feature tight tracking to mirror military heads-up navigation displays, while all numeric figures must invoke tabular alignment figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent jittering during active real-time data streaming.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses a radial-concentric HUD anchor combined with an orthographic 12-column peripheral telemetry grid. 

- **Primary Desktop Viewport (1440px+):** The viewport is centered around an orbital focal hub housing cognitive progress metrics, dynamic knowledge vector charts, and neural state indicators. Peripheral wings hold technical module tracks: system stream on the left, interactive dialogue and knowledge breakdown on the right.
- **Adaptive Reflow:** On viewports below 1024px, the radial HUD docks to the top third of the viewport as an elliptical compressed header gauge, converting the peripheral rails into a vertically stacked sequence. At mobile break (<640px), radial geometry collapses into a streamlined segmented horizontal ribbon with micro-dials.
- **Rhythm & Grid:** An absolute 4px/8px micro-pitch governs internal component spacing. Structural panels are separated by fixed 16px gutters, reinforced by 1px alignment axes that slice through the canvas like targeting crosshairs.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation is rendered through chromatic luminescence and surface opacity rather than drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Deep Substratum):** Solid `#090B0D` canvas backdrop overlaid with optional 24px micro-grid cross-dots at 15% opacity.
- **Level 1 (Telemetry Chassis):** Dark graphite `#111519` with 70% opacity, bordered by 1px solid `#1E2830`.
- **Level 2 (Active Gauges & Scopes):** Elevated containers utilizing `#171D22` at 85% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, and hairline edges framed in `#263844`.
- **Active State Illumination:** Interactive focus creates an inner electric aura rather than a standard blur shadow: `box-shadow: inset 0 0 12px rgba(0, 217, 255, 0.15), 0 0 8px rgba(0, 217, 255, 0.3)`.
- **Warning & Divergence Elevation:** Amber channels trigger localized warm alerts via `0 0 10px rgba(215, 166, 90, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary rejects pill-like and generic soft rounded aesthetics, enforcing technical precision:

- **Orthogonal Geometry:** Base containers feature pure 90-degree corners (`roundedness: 0`), reinforced with mechanical chamfers (45-degree angle cuts at 6px to 10px) on exterior diagnostic viewports.
- **Concentric Arcs & Radial Segments:** Circular dials feature precise orbital paths, split rings, dynamic dashed strokes (`stroke-dasharray`), and segmented angular gaps.
- **Framing Brackets:** Content blocks are punctuated by corner tick marks (2px L-shaped corner indicators) and micro reticles instead of rounded borders, creating the sensation of active hardware scanning frames.

## Components

### Buttons & Tactical Triggers
- **Primary Action:** Solid electric cyan border (`#00D9FF`), black substrate (`#090B0D`), high-contrast cyan label in `JetBrains Mono` bold uppercase. Hover activates a cyan sweep fill (`background: #00D9FF`, text: `#090B0D`) accompanied by a subtle `0 0 14px rgba(0, 217, 255, 0.6)` glow.
- **Secondary Segment:** Ghost chassis border (`#263844`), text `#8EA7B5`. Hover shifts border to `#79E8FF` with a translucent tint fill (`rgba(0, 217, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Corner Clippings:** Tactical buttons utilize a 4px chamfered top-right and bottom-left corner cut.

### Diagnostic Chips & Status Flags
- Scaled to 20px height, monospaced 10px uppercase text.
- Outlined with 1px border; prepended with a 4px pulsating LED pip (cyan for nominal tutor state, amber for critical review required).
- No rounded pills; pure rectangular or chevron-ended bounding contours.

### Peripheral Lists & Telemetry Logs
- Flush left alignment with micro line numbers in `#4D6270`.
- Data rows delimited by 1px dashed rules (`#1E2830`).
- Hover highlights the entire row with a faint horizontal gradient wash (`linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(0,217,255,0.08) 0%, transparent 100%)`) and turns the active indicator pip electric cyan.

### Form Inputs & Terminal Command Fields
- Inset dark background (`#0A0D10`), single crisp bottom border in `#263844` with L-shaped corner brackets on left and right edges.
- Typing activates an electric cyan baseline with a blinking square block cursor (`▋`).
- Placeholder text in JetBrains Mono at 40% cyan-gray opacity (`#4D6270`).

### Selection Controls (Checks & Switches)
- **Checkboxes:** Pure square with inverted cyan center block when checked; active state produces an electric cyan crosshair or center node.
- **Radio Nodes:** Concentric dual-ring reticle; active selection illuminates the inner core circle surrounded by a rotating segmented dash circle.

### Orbital HUD Widgets & Radial Dials
- Concentric circular SVG components displaying mastery velocity, comprehension bandwidth, and retention curves.
- Outer ring houses calibrated degree ticks and alphanumeric indexing.
- Center displays high-contrast dynamic values in `Manrope` with monospaced units (`JetBrains Mono`).

### Telemetry Cards & Viewports
- Flat `#111519` backing bordered by 1px `#1E2830`.
- Top header includes an integrated technical badge bar, channel label (e.g., `// MOD: QUANTUM_LOGIC_01`), and corner status coordinates.
